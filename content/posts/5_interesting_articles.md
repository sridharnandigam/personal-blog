### The US Constitution
I further added to my book collection when I found *The US Constitution and Other Writings* for sale at Costco.

The book is a compilation of important writings and speeches throughout US history.

I have found that legislation is actually pretty interesting to read and Ben Franklin really had quite the sharp tongue.

Also, it seems that they didn't have a habit using punctuation back then. 
They just separated everything with semicolons and paragraphs so reading some of this legislation was a nightmare.

All in all, it's really interesting to see how the language of American politics has evolved over the centuries.
I definitely recommend reading this if you need a refresher of American history.
