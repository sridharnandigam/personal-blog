Building Robots That Can Do Anything - Dr. Chelsea Finn

The above blog article is written by a researcher at Physical Intelligence and references the company’s work quite a bit.

My first introduction to Physical Intelligence was through this video I saw a few months ago and it’s definitely worth revisiting.

Dr. Chelsea Finn, research lead and co-founder, discusses why robotics is still not quite in the spotlight despite the current AI boom.

The TLDR is that we lack sufficient usable training data for robots. Now that might seem like an obvious statement but the problem goes incredibly deep, which is why this research is so meaningful.

Many complain that they would rather have AI do their laundry and other daily chores rather than generate art. Dr. Finn’s research tackled the very problem of folding laundry.

Look Back - Tatsuki Fujimoto

My first physical manga copy. I enjoyed the Chainsaw Man movie so much that I decided to see what else Tatsuki Fujimoto has written.

It’s a wonderful story about the hard work and pain that goes into being an artist, great or otherwise. Fujimoto really knows how to make the most of the manga format to tell a cinematic story. My favorite example of this is how he showed the passage of time and the protaganist toiling through each day to master her craft. There are many more instances

I’m looking forward to watching the movie when I get the chance.

The US Constitution

I further added to my book collection when I found The US Constitution and Other Writings for sale at Costco.

The book is a compilation of important writings and speeches throughout US history.

I have found that legislation is actually pretty interesting to read and that Ben Franklin really knew how to diss the British.

Also, it seems that they didn’t have punctuation back then. They just separated everything with semicolons and paragraphs. Trying to read the Dawes Act was a damn nightmare (that may have been on purpose though).
